<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>
  This project involved the design and fabrication of a solenoid-driven rotary
  motor, together with dynamic modeling, closed-loop control design, simulation,
  and experimental validation.
</p>

<h2>Contributions</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Designed and fabricated a solenoid-actuated rotary-motor mechanism.</li>
  <li>Developed a dynamic model of the electromechanical system.</li>
  <li>Implemented a proportional-integral (PI) controller for closed-loop speed regulation.</li>
  <li>Evaluated system behavior through simulation and experimental measurements.</li>
  <li>Analyzed the effect of controller parameters on speed-tracking performance.</li>
</ul>
