## Overview

This project involved the design and fabrication of a solenoid-driven rotary motor, along with dynamic modeling and closed-loop speed control.

## Contributions

- Designed a solenoid-actuated rotary-motor mechanism.
- Developed a dynamic model of the electromechanical system.
- Implemented a PI controller for closed-loop speed regulation.
- Conducted simulation and experimental validation.
